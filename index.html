Windows OS Gesture Controls Cheatsheet

Full gesture guide for real-time mouse, scroll, media, window, and desktop control

Cursor Steering Mouse

Point your Index finger towards the camera. The cursor smoothly follows your fingertip across all monitors.

Tips: Keep other fingers comfortably relaxed or curled.
Left Click & Double Click Mouse

Quickly pinch Thumb and Index finger tips together and release within 350ms. Pinch twice quickly for Double Click.

Tips: A green reticle flash confirms the left click.
Left Drag & Drop Mouse

Pinch and hold Thumb + Index for >380ms. The cursor locks in mouse-down state. Move your hand to drag windows or files, then release to drop.

Tips: The action badge turns amber and displays "DRAGGING".
Right Click (Context Menu) Mouse

Pinch your Thumb tip and Middle finger tip together. Opens context menus on desktop, files, and web pages.

Tips: Distinct from Left Click to eliminate accidental clicks.
Smooth Scrolling Navigation

Extend both Index and Middle fingers upright side-by-side (peace sign closed) and move your hand up or down to scroll web pages and documents.

Tips: Continuous fluid motion smoothly rotates the mouse wheel.
Master Volume Control Media

Adjust distance between Thumb and Index tips (aperture dial) to set volume 0–100%, or hold Thumb Up / Thumb Down for incremental steps.

Tips: Real-time volume percentage is reflected in the HUD.
Screen Brightness Level Display

Hold 3 fingers upright (Index, Middle, Ring - like 'W' / '3') and elevate hand up or down to adjust your monitor's hardware brightness level.

Tips: Higher hand position raises brightness, lower lowers it.
Instant Screenshot & Snip Utility

Form an "L" sign (Thumb + Index at 90°) and hold steady for 1.2s to trigger Windows Snipping Tool (Win+Shift+S) or full screenshot saved to Pictures.

Tips: Progress circle animates around reticle while holding.
Show Desktop Window

Push your Flat Open Palm downward rapidly towards bottom edge of the frame to minimize all windows and show desktop (Win+D).

Tips: Perform the gesture again to restore windows.
Virtual Keyboard Typing Input

Click OSK Keyboard on the HUD or use the letter recognizer to type into any active Windows application, text field, or notepad.

Tips: Direct text injection sends keystrokes with 0 delay.
